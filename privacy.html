Your data, plainly explained

Dopamine Calendar (beta) · Last updated 17 November 2026
In short: Dopamine Calendar has no server. Everything you enter goes straight from your phone into your own Google account. We never receive, see, copy or sell your data.

2. Signing in with Google

When you sign in, Google gives the app a temporary access key. The app uses your primary calendar’s address to show which Google account your data goes into. The access key is kept only in your browser on your device.

3. Your calendar and to-dos

The app reads and writes your events in Google Calendar, and your Top 3, deadlines and week notes in Google Tasks. This happens directly between your device and Google. Purpose: to show and edit your calendar. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app you chose to use).

4. Migraine log (health data)

Only if you turn it on. The app saves when attacks start and end, their phase, aura type and medication in a private calendar called “My tracking” in your Google account. Symptoms and medication are stored in hidden fields of each entry, not in its title. Purpose: to show your migraine days, adapt your day and create a report for your doctor. Legal basis: your explicit consent, Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

While an attack is going on, the app also keeps the current attack in your browser’s local storage on your device, so nothing gets lost if you are offline.

5. Settings on your device

Your display settings (such as mode and brightness level) and the names of medications you logged are saved in your browser’s local storage. We use no tracking cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

6. Hosting

The app’s files are delivered by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open the app, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the files and may log it for security. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (secure delivery of the app). Details: GitHub’s privacy statement.

7. About Google

Your Google account is your own service, and Google’s privacy policy applies to it. Google says it does not use Calendar content for advertising. Exception: if you turn on “Personal Intelligence” and connect Calendar, Google may use that content to personalise content and ads. You can disconnect it in your Google Account under Data & privacy.

8. How long data is kept – and deleting it

Your data stays in your Google account for as long as you keep it there. “Delete all health data” in the app’s settings removes the Health calendar and everything in it. You can remove the app’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent for the migraine log at any time in the app’s settings. This does not affect anything that happened before you withdrew it.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict your data, to data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Because your data is in your own Google account, you can do most of this yourself. For anything else, write to us. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA).

11. Not a medical device

Dopamine Calendar is a calendar with a personal log. It does not diagnose, assess symptoms or give medical advice. In an emergency, call 112.

12. Changes

If this information changes, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
